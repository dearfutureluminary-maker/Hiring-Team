Built by Youth,
for Youth.

Not because it sounds nice on a poster.

Because I genuinely needed this website when I was younger.

I spent years discovering opportunities completely by accident. A random Instagram post. A friend sharing a link. A deadline I almost missed. A program I found at 1 a.m. while scrolling through page seven of Google.

Every opportunity opened another door. But finding them always felt harder than it should have been. So instead of keeping everything buried in screenshots, bookmarks, and notes I'd never look at again... I put it here.

What You'll
Find Here

Personal
Growth

Opportunities are great. But they're way more useful when you know how to communicate your ideas, lead a team, handle rejection, stay curious, and believe you're capable of more than you think.

Nobody is born knowing how to do that. The good news? You can learn.

Global
Opportunities

The internet is full of incredible programs. The problem is that they're scattered everywhere.

One is hidden on a university website. Another is buried in someone's LinkedIn post. A third disappears before most students even hear about it.

This section exists to make those opportunities easier to find—and harder to miss.

Clear
Roadmaps

One of the most frustrating feelings in the world is wanting to learn something and having no clue where to start.

Coding. Research. Medicine. Sustainability. Content creation. YouTube tells you one thing. Google tells you another. Everyone has an opinion.

These roadmaps cut through the noise and give you an actual starting point.

Profile
Building

At some point, you'll need to introduce yourself. On a CV. In an application. On LinkedIn. Maybe even in an interview.

And somehow nobody teaches that in school. This section does.

The
Founder

Hi, I'm Malak.

And Dear Future Luminary started with a question I couldn't stop asking:

"What if finding opportunities was a little less confusing?"

Over the years, I joined programs, met inspiring people, traveled virtually across the world, learned new skills, and discovered paths I never knew existed. Not because I had some special advantage. Mostly because I kept searching. A lot.

And eventually I realized that thousands of students were searching for the exact same things. So I built the resource I wish I'd had from the beginning.
